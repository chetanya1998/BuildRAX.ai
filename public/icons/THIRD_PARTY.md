# Component icon sources

- AI semantic icons in `figma-node-icons.svg` are the exact local SVG export supplied for BuildRAX.
- The remaining semantic component glyphs are rendered locally from the installed
  `lucide-react` package. Lucide is distributed under the ISC license, with icons
  derived from Feather covered by MIT: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE

No draw.io or Eraser stencil/catalog artwork is copied into this repository.
